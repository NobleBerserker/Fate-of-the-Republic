The God of Death governs the inevitable — not with malice, but with solemn grace. War, order, and arcane forces are instruments of finality. Their clerics act as death’s judges, scholars of forgotten magic, twilight guardians, and field chaplains who guide souls from battle into peace. They stand at the threshold and ensure it is respected.

Domains: Death, Grave, War, Twilight, Knowledge, Order, Arcana
