The God of Life embodies all that grows, heals, and creates. Though peaceful and nurturing, they also encompass the wild chaos of nature and the power of storms. Even trickery is seen as a tool of life’s adaptability. Their clerics often serve as healers, druids, elemental sages, peacemakers, and joyful wanderers who spark change where there is stagnation.

**Domains:** Life, Nature, Light, Peace, Tempest, Forge, Trickery
