The [[Group|group]] discussed what to do next. Some were unsure that they had to keep pursuing this. Others, could not justify going against [[Ralindra Veyn|Ralindra]]. In the end, [[Eldrin Duskbane|Eldrin]] and [[Rowan]] decided to do some more questioning in the [[Stonemarket]], [[Bramble Bast|Bramble]] and [[Liam Jasper Locke|Liam]] would attend the [[Ralindra Veyn|Ralindra]]'s post funeral gathering at her [[Ralindra's Home|house]] while [[Aurelia Blackbriar|Aurelia]] checked the Adventurer's guild [[18-2 Quest Board|board]] to keep their options open. 

[[Eldrin Duskbane|Eldrin]] and [[Rowan]] learned that the [[Ashen Fangs]] is not as accepted in the [[Stonemarket]] as they were in [[Weaver’s Rest]] but they could not find anything truly terrible. They stole from trader's, shopkeeper and stole an [[Amulet of Proof against Detection and Location|amulet]] that belonged to a widow's husband. They also asked about the [[Grey Hand|Grey Hands]], the people were afraid to reply, especially when asked about [[Mask]]. Lastly they learned about [[Darin Hallowmere]], it seemed that the people were glad he was leading the [[Republic of Bridgehold|Republic]]. Afterwards they proceeded to talk with the [[First Steward]] and learned that there is an official investigation led by the [[Councilor of Justice]].

[[Bramble Bast|Bramble]] and [[Liam Jasper Locke|Liam]] reached Ralindra's home. They found the [[Ashen Fangs]] and [['One-Eyed' Jordan Trollsbane|One-eyed]] gathered to pay condolences. [[Bramble Bast|Bramble]] distracted them while [[Liam Jasper Locke|Liam]] searched the house, he felt something like a trapdoor under the carpet in the living room. He also searched two of the rooms. In one of them he found a [[Veyn Wedding Ring|golden ring]]. In the other room he found a [[Ashen Fang (Dagger)|dagger]]. He pocketed them both. His investigation was interrupted by another assassination attempt. This time against [[Ralindra Veyn]], [[Garrik 'The Ox' Durrow|Garrik]] got hit in her place and [['One-Eyed' Jordan Trollsbane|'One-eyed']] made sure he survives.

[[Aurelia Blackbriar|Aurelia]] found several opportunities but she was unimpressed by the rewards. After discussion the group decided to help the [[Copper League]] with the [[Catacombs of Eltathar|Gnawing Catacombs]] beneath the [[Old Mine]].
