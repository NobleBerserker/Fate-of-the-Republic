The [[Group|group]] succeeded in fighting the skeletons by simply putting the gold back. When they returned the stolen gold, the dead went to rest once more. The [[Group|group]] decided not to move further into the sarcophagi chambers. Instead, they moved into a hall where the tombs of the [[Eltathar Dynasty]] could be found.

[[House Draev]]'s tomb was decorated with armor and weapons. [[House Morveign]]'s tomb was supposedly guarded by a gargoyle. [[House Valcaryn]]'s tomb was adorned with statues wearing actual armor. Behind two of the statues were ghouls—they attacked Liam, but through a group effort, he was rescued, and the ghouls were trapped inside. Last was the magically dark [[House Veymar]]'s tomb.

At the end of the hall stood a huge door, decorated with the seals of the Houses. It appeared to be the tomb of the last king, [[Valdrion Eltathar]]. Oozing through the edges of the door was a strange reddish substance. Rowan inspected it, and some got on him, but luckily, it didn’t affect him much. The voices in his head demanded, **"RELEASE ME."**

Considering their options, the [[Group|group]] decided they had done enough. They returned and reported the information they gathered to the [[Dale Hartwick|Foreman]]. They also informed the [[Chronicler]]'s office. As it seemed, the [[Old Mine]] would be closed for a while.

Back at [[Bannershire]], the [[Group|group]] considered their next steps.
