The [[Group|group]] was ready to pursue a new quest, except [[Eldrin Duskbane]], who said he was needed at the [[Military Quarter]]. They decided to find [[Jarrik Holt]]. They learned that [[Jarrik Holt]] is a gambler with a great debt to the [[Black Harriers]], an organization in the [[Hearthside]]. The group went to speak with [[Jarrik Holt|Jarrik]]'s sister [[Griselda Holt|Griselda]]. [[Griselda Holt|Griselda]], a merchant like [[Jarrik Holt|Jarrik]], but more reserved and with her own store, met the group at her candle shop, the [[Hearth & Hollow]]. She told them about [[Jarrik Holt|Jarrik]]'s debt to a loan shark named [[Vaughn Hargrave]]. The last time she saw [[Jarrik Holt|Jarrik]], he was going to [[Ember Dice]] to try his luck. But this was three days ago. Griselda also allowed them to search [[Jarrik Holt|Jarrik]]'s house, where they found a ledger that mentioned a clear orb he bought and sold. The orb had shown him his house at [[Brightmarch]].

The group visited [[Ember Dice]] in the same afternoon. The place was filled with people gambling away their money. The group tried some gambling too, and [[Rowan]] made a great sum as he tried a high-stakes table. The important thing was that they got to kindly question [[Vaughn Hargrave]]. He said that [[Jarrik Holt|Jarrik]] was particularly lucky, and he had managed to pay him back and even earn some more. A [[Severa Lys|silver-eyed woman]] sat next to him. Even the district's gossip specialist [[Tom Rancher|Tom]] didn't know the woman, but he knew that she and [[Jarrik Holt|Jarrik]] had gone to an abandoned warehouse together. "Of course! I would too!" Tom said as he led the group to the warehouse. The group knew that she didn't take Jarrik there for a good cause. So they told Tom to leave as they entered the warehouse. After some searching, they found the basement, and [[Jarrik Holt|Jarrik]] was there, in a dark cell. His memory had been tampered with, and one thing he did not remember was the [[Orb of Superior Scrying|Orb]].  On her final glance at the room, [[Aurelia Blackbriar|Aurelia]] saw a familiar square spiral, with just the name, [[Severa Lys]].

The group, thinking that the house in [[Brightmarch]] was the key to solving this mystery, convinced Jarrik to give them an additional 50gp so they would escort him to [[Brightmarch]]. Less than a day away, the group quickly learned that the house was not what the silver-eyed woman was after; maybe it was the orb.

Now in Brightmarch, the group is waiting for [[Eldrin Duskbane|Eldrin]]. Maybe there is something interesting in Brightmarch's Adventurer's Guild. Maybe [[Eldrin Duskbane|Eldrin]] learned something in the [[Military Quarter]].
