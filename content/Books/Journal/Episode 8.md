Since [[Redmere]] and the [[Redleaf Orchard|Orchard]] were so close by, the [[Group]] decided to assist [[Gullen Batwick]] in locating his missing foreman, [[Corwin Miller|Corwin]]. Upon arriving, they spoke with [[Gullen Batwick|Gullen]] and learned that [[Corwin Miller|Corwin]] had vanished shortly after expressing suspicion that thieves were sneaking into the [[Redleaf Orchard|Orchard]] at night.

Unbeknownst to Gullen, Corwin had accidentally uncovered an ancient [[altar of the Cult of Many|altar]] once used by the [[Cult of Many]]. The lingering remnants of sacrificial victims—manifesting as shadows—attacked him, dragging his spirit into the afterlife. The reactivation of the [[altar of the Cult of Many|altar]] somehow alerted nearby cultists, who rushed to the site. In an effort to conceal what had transpired, they retrieved Corwin's body and brought it to their hideout.

The [[Group]] tracked the cultists' movements, eventually discovering their lair. A swift and decisive assault allowed them to eliminate the cultists. During the rescue, they also managed to save a local [[rescued villager|villager]] native to [[Redmere]].
