It was time to explore the [[Old Mine]]. The [[Group|group]] finished their last-minute preparations and left [[Bannershire]] via the southern road. The mine was only a few hours away. When they arrived, they hailed the guard, who led them to the Foreman, [[Dale Hartwick]].

They found [[Dale Hartwick|Dale]] having a heated discussion with a representative of the [[Chronicler]]'s office, [[Sedric Vance]]. The Foreman simply wanted the problem in the mine gone, while the Chronicler wanted to shut down the mine, clear it, and then conduct an archaeological examination of the site to ensure that all historical information was carefully extracted.

The [[Group|group]] had a private conversation with the Foreman, and he told them that he wouldn’t mind if the [[Chronicler]] did not find a reason to explore further within the mines. However, he could not fulfill the [[Group|group]]'s demands for additional payment.

As they approached the [[Catacombs of Eltathar|catacombs]], they were attacked by swarms of rats. The rats were surprisingly aggressive, but the [[Group|group]] defeated them. Now even more cautious, they proceeded toward the [[Catacombs of Eltathar|catacombs]]. [[Bramble Bast]] seemed to be holding a conversation with [[Pickles the Rat]], which [[Rowan]] had captured. The [[Pickles the Rat|rat]] seemingly convinced him to approach a group of [[Aberrat|giant rats]] and pet them. Unsurprisingly, the rats did not share [[Bramble Bast|Bramble]]'s friendliness. The [[Group|group]] had to strike down a few of them until the rest dispersed.

With that done, the [[Group|group]] proceeded to the Catacombs, where [[Liam Jasper Locke|Liam]] unlocked the door, and they pushed right through. Inside the Catacombs, [[Aurelia Blackbriar|Aurelia]] and [[Eldrin Duskbane|Eldrin]] came to a disagreement. [[Aurelia Blackbriar|Aurelia]] wanted to recover treasure from the sarcophagi, while [[Eldrin Duskbane|Eldrin]] considered the act sacrilege. The looting of the sarcophagi triggered the rise of several skeletal warriors, and it was uncertain what would happen next.
