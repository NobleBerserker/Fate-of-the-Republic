It was time to interrogate the captured [[Dark Blades]]. The [[Group|group]] began by questioning the thief first, but he could not provide much information. He had no knowledge of why [[Yarek 'The Collector']] had a letter from 'R,' and the claim that this would be [[Ralindra Veyn]] seemed unsubstantial. [[Yarek 'The Collector']] also appeared confused about the letter—or perhaps he was simply a skilled liar.

The interrogation took longer than expected, and it became clear that the [[Dark Blades]] had skilled trackers, as they quickly discovered the alley where the [[Group|group]] was hiding. Enter [[Ralvo Marlow]], the [[Dark Blades]]' boss, followed by a crew of gang members and [[Nyla Thorne]]. [[Aurelia Blackbriar|Aurelia]] cast _Mage Hand_ and floated the letter to [[Ralvo Marlow|Ralvo]], hoping his reaction would provide insight into the situation. [[Ralvo Marlow|Ralvo]] handed the letter to [[Nyla Thorne|Nyla]], who examined it and confirmed that the handwriting did not belong to [[Yarek 'The Collector'|Yarek]] or [[Ralindra Veyn|Ralindra]].

[[Ralvo Marlow|Ralvo]] shared what he knew about Ralindra, leading to an argument with [[Eldrin Duskbane|Eldrin]] about 'justice.' To settle the matter, [[Ralvo Marlow|Ralvo]] proposed solving it with iron. He proved stronger than [[Eldrin Duskbane|Eldrin]] had expected, but thanks to the duel, the [[Group|group]] was free to go.

To cover more ground, the [[Group|group]] decided to split up. [[Aurelia Blackbriar|Aurelia]], [[Bramble Bast|Bramble]], and [[Eldrin Duskbane|Eldrin]] stayed at [[Weaver’s Rest]] to learn more about [[Ralindra Veyn|Ralindra]], her [[Granny Ralindra|grandmother]], and the [[Ashen Fangs]]. Meanwhile, [[Liam Jasper Locke|Liam]] and [[Rowan|Rowan]] headed to the cemetery to attend the funeral, hoping to gather more information.

At [[Weaver’s Rest]], they uncovered rumors about [[Ralindra Veyn|Ralindra]] and the [[Ashen Fangs|Fangs]]. The gang had once stolen a single letter from a [[Luke Varnholt|merchant]], forcing him to flee [[Bannershire]], and had also looted silk from [[Henry Dauvrey|another merchant]]'s warehouse—only to distribute it among local tailors. Regarding [[Ralindra Veyn|Ralindra]] herself, they learned of a recent violent outburst against a [[Cult of Many|group of people]] who had been terrorizing some [[Street Rats|orphans]]. As for her [[Granny Ralindra|grandmother]], she seemed to be widely admired.

At the funeral, [[Liam Jasper Locke|Liam]] and [[Rowan|Rowan]] spotted [[Ralindra Veyn|Ralindra]] surrounded by the [[Ashen Fangs]]. Among those who spoke with her were [['One-Eyed' Jordan Trollsbane|One-Eyed]] and [[Viktor 'Mask' Roscoe|Viktor Roscoe]], the [[Councilor of Foreign Affairs]]. Intrigued, they listened closely and discovered that the [[Councilor of Foreign Affairs|Councilor]] had known her grandmother.

After he departed, they approached [[Ralindra Veyn|Ralindra]] to offer their condolences. Through their conversation, they learned about [[The Iron Pact]], an adventuring party that played a crucial role in the [[The Peasant Revolution of Bridgehold]] and the [[Troll Wars]]. The group disbanded after the [[Troll Wars]] following the early death of [[Gareth Holloway]].

Based on [[Viktor 'Mask' Roscoe|Viktor Roscoe]]’s words to [[Ralindra Veyn|Ralindra]], it seemed her grandmother had been a good person—despite the [[Nyxis|darkness]] that had followed her.
