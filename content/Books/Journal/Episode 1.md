**[[Aurelia Blackbriar]]** sat at her desk when a familiar chill swept through the room. A message from her patron appeared, twisting into a spiral of infernal symbols. As always, the words were cryptic, but the meaning was clear:

_"The next target is [[Ralindra Veyn]]."_

[[Aurelia Blackbriar]]’s mission was to go to **[[Councilor’s Square]]**, where she would follow an assassin who would lead her to her target. With no time to waste, she gathered her things, ready to pursue the next phase of her deadly task.

**[[Bramble Bast]]** was down in the basement with his wife, cleaning up the mess from last night’s heavy rain. The basement had flooded, and the cluttered space was now filled with water. As [[Bramble Bast]] moved things around carelessly, he caused a bit more damage, which led to a heated argument with his wife. Frustrated, she told him to leave and head to **[[Councilor’s Square]]**, where the **[[First Steward]]** would be making an important announcement.

**[[Rowan]]** was drinking at the **Heart of [[Bannershire]]**, a tavern in **[[Councilor’s Square]]**, when he overheard the locals gossiping about the upcoming speech from the **[[First Steward]]**. Curiosity piqued, he decided to head out and see for himself what the speech would be about.

**[[Eldrin Duskbane]]**, a guest at the **[[Bannershire]] Barracks**, was busy shining his armor when [[Sergeant Galen Voss]] suggested he accompany the unit to **[[Councilor’s Square]]**. The [[First Steward]] was about to give a speech, and it would be a great chance to observe the Republic's culture up close. Seeing it as an opportunity to uncover any corruption within the Republic, [[Eldrin Duskbane]] agreed. He joined the unit, positioning himself in a place where he could keep an eye out for any potential threats.

**[[Liam Jasper Locke]]** was in the middle of a card game at **[[The Rusty Coin]]**, a bar in the rough Farthings, when an affiliate known as **[[Dorian 'Scales' Viera]]** interrupted. Scales delivered a message: "They" had a mission for him. He reminded [[Liam Jasper Locke]] of the [[Ashen Fangs]]' recent disrespect towards "Them," and it was time for retaliation. The plan was set, and all Liam had to do was ensure everything went smoothly. So, he made his way to **[[Councilor’s Square]]** to witness the plan unfold.

As the characters gathered in **Councilor's Square**, a sudden assassination attempt unfolded before their eyes. The **[[First Steward]]** was targeted, but two **[[Crimson Knight]]s** stood guard. One of them, with a cold, piercing gaze, turned towards the assassin, sword in hand. The assassin froze for a moment before bolting into a frantic escape.

[[Aurelia Blackbriar]] and [[Eldrin Duskbane]] quickly gave chase, determined to stop him. Bramble, with his loud voice, muttered about how back in his day an assassin wouldn’t get away so easily. Liam, taking a more cautious approach, decided to wait and watch to see what the guards would do next. Meanwhile, Rowan, unfazed, continued to listen to the **[[First Steward]]'s** speech as if nothing had happened — the [[First Steward]] himself ignoring the attack and pressing on with his address.

[[Aurelia Blackbriar]] and [[Eldrin Duskbane]] caught the assassin, learning his name was **[[Krevik Dallor]]**. His dark orange bandana marked him as a member of the **[[Ashen Fangs]]**. After questioning him, they suspected that [[Krevik Dallor]] had been under a mind-affecting spell at the time of the attack. He mentioned a stranger he met at **[[Monocular]]**, a tavern in **[[Weaver’s Rest]]**, just before deciding to carry out the assassination.

Deciding to investigate further, they took [[Krevik Dallor]] to **[[Gallows Lane]]**, where the **[[Guardhouse]]** was located. To their surprise, they found **[[Bramble Bast]]** in a cell, detained for reasons unknown. The guards called for a mage from **[[Highspire]]** to examine [[Krevik Dallor]] for any magical influence. After a thorough investigation, it was determined that no magic had left a trace on him. Despite this, the guards decided to take **[[Bramble Bast]]** along and set off for **[[Monocular]]** to continue their search.

For their own reasons, **[[Rowan]]** and **[[Liam Jasper Locke]]** also made their way to **[[Monocular]]**, where they ended up conversing. Liam, subtly on the lookout for anyone to target from the **[[Ashen Fangs]]**, chatted with Rowan, all while scanning the room.

Just as their conversation was unfolding, **[[Bramble Bast]]**, **[[Aurelia Blackbriar]]**, and **[[Eldrin Duskbane]]** entered. **[[Eldrin Duskbane]]** immediately asked, loudly, if anyone had information about the attack on the **[[First Steward]]**. The question sent a wave of tension through the tavern, with every drunkard reaching for their weapon. However, the **one-eyed tavern owner** of **[[Monocular]]** quickly defused the situation, offering a free round of drinks to calm the crowd.

With the tension eased, **[[Eldrin Duskbane]]** approached the owner, determined to get answers. He began questioning the tavern keeper about the mysterious stranger [[Krevik Dallor]] had met right before the assassination attempt.

**[[Liam Jasper Locke]]** joined **[[Eldrin Duskbane]]** and **[[Bramble Bast]]** at the bar, and together they learned that **[[Krevik Dallor]]** had been talking to a masked man known on the streets as **[[Viktor 'Mask' Roscoe]]**. Before they could fully process this, the door to **[[Monocular]]** swung open. In walked five members of the **[[Ashen Fangs]]**, one of them being none other than **[[Ralindra Veyn]]**. The owner greeted her warmly.
