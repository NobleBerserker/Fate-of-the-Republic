The **Iron Pact** is an old adventuring party. Its members played a crucial role in aiding the [[Duchy of Bridgehold]] through numerous challenges, including the [[Troll Wars]]. Ultimately, they assisted in overthrowing the duke, leading to the dissolution of the [[Duchy of Bridgehold]] and the rise of the [[Republic of Bridgehold]].
