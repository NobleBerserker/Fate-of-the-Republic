Bridgehold is governed by the elected [[Council of Banners]], led by the [[First Steward]]. The Republic was formed after the overthrow of the [[Duchy of Bridgehold]]. Each town and settlement sent a representative to the new government.

See [[Bannershire]] for the city’s districts and local politics, and [[The Peasant Revolution of Bridgehold]] and the [[Troll Wars]] for the history people still talk about.
