Located beneath an old, long-abandoned [[Old Mine|mine]] outside [[Bannershire]], the **Catacombs of Eltathar** are the final resting place of the long-lost **Eltathar Dynasty**. Originally built as tombs for the noble families who ruled the land, these catacombs have remained sealed for centuries. However, recent activity—copper mining in the area—has caused disturbances that have awakened things best left undisturbed.

The catacombs consist of **four tombs**, each one belonging to a different house of the dynasty. At the end of the catacombs was a huge door, oozing with a weird red substance.
