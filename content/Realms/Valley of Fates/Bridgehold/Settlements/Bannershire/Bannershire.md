A longtime resident knows where to shop, who keeps order, and which streets have a reputation. News travels through taverns, market stalls, and the Adventurer’s Guild noticeboard. How much you hear depends on where you spend your time.

## Who has influence

- **[[Council of Banners]].** The elected council governs the [[Republic of Bridgehold]]. [[Darin Hallowmere]], the First Steward, is well liked around town. Marshal [[Lorian Veymont]] leads the army, and [[Tessa Ambrith]] has a reputation as a hard-line judge.
- **[[Iron Guard]].** Its patrols and curfews are familiar. It champions a stronger military and strict enforcement of the Republic’s laws.
- **[[Verdant Circle]].** Keeper of Grain [[Maerin Greenfield]] is associated with this group, which calls for protecting farmland and forests from overuse.
- **Silent Banner.** It favors the old ways. People who follow politics sometimes speculate that it wants a new duchy.
- **[[Copper League]] and [[Greythorne Trading Company]].** The League is a commercial organization with ties to mining and infrastructure. Greythorne sends Valley goods out through Driftport to the Sundering Sea.

## Street talk

You’re more likely to hear these stories in the neighborhoods involved. Accounts vary from one street to the next.

- **Ashen Fangs.** In Weaver’s Rest, some say the gang once handed stolen silk to local tailors. Traders in Stonemarket tend to remember the thefts. The gang is associated with orange face coverings.
- **Dark Blades.** The Fangs’ rivals in Weaver’s Rest are associated with a black dagger mark. People talk about being made to pay a “toll” to use certain alleys.
- **Black Harriers.** Their name comes up around Hearthside when people talk about gambling debts and loan sharks.
- **Grey Hand.** A name whispered in connection with smuggling and protection money. Asking too openly about the group can end a conversation.
- **Street Rats.** There are stories of a gang of children in Bannershire.

## History and faith

Older residents remember the grain warehouse fire and the unrest that brought down the old duchy. The [[The Peasant Revolution of Bridgehold|Peasant Revolution]] gave Bridgehold its elected council. The [[The Iron Pact|Iron Pact]] is remembered for its part in both the [[Troll Wars]] and the overthrow of the duke.

The Valley’s pantheon includes [[Veylinn]], god of life, and [[Veyrith]], god of death. The three [[Fates]] govern the thread of a life. [[Clotho]] spins it, [[Lachesis]] measures it, and [[Atropos]] cuts it.

## News in town

- **The attack in Councilor’s Square.** During a public speech, someone tried to assassinate First Steward Darin Hallowmere. Two Crimson Knights were guarding him. The attacker fled, and Hallowmere continued his address.
