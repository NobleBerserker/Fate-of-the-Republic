### Reputation & Rumors

- **"Untouchable"** – Attempts to expose or remove him have always ended in failure. Accusers either recant, disappear, or become his allies.
- **"He Has No Friends, Only Tools"** – Many say 'Mask' views people as nothing more than pieces on a board, to be used and discarded as needed.
