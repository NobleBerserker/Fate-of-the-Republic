**Tharlen Veymont** is the younger son of [[Duke Alric Veymont]], the last Duke of Bannershire, and the brother of [[Lorian Veymont]]. Unlike his elder brother, who dedicated his life to warfare, Tharlen always favored diplomacy, commerce, and political maneuvering.

Following the fall of his father's regime and the establishment of the [[Republic of Bridgehold]], Tharlen built a new life in the [[Kingdom of Aeloris]], where he now owns and operates a successful banking company.

A shrewd businessman and skilled negotiator, Tharlen has cultivated connections among merchants, nobles, and influential figures throughout Aeloris. Though he rarely involves himself openly in the affairs of his homeland, his family's history and his considerable financial influence make him a figure worth watching.
