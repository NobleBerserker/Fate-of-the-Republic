### **Reputation & Rumors:**

- **"The Widow of Ashes"** – Some say she orchestrated her own husband’s death decades ago to claim control of the family. Others whisper that she has never truly mourned anyone.
- **"A Pact Unfulfilled"** – There are whispers that Ophelia made a deal with a dark entity long ago, and though she hasn’t paid her price yet, the debt still lingers.
- **"Her Smile is a Warning"** – Those who have tried to cross Ophelia often find their fortunes turned to dust—without ever knowing how it happened.
