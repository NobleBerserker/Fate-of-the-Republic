A political organization in the [[Republic of Bridgehold]].

### Philosophy
Strength and order safeguard freedom. A disciplined blade is the shield of the republic.

### Methods
- **Strict Law Enforcement** – They patrol the streets, enforce curfews, and crack down on crime with ruthless efficiency.
- **Surveillance & Informants** – They maintain an extensive network of spies and informants to monitor potential threats.
- **Intimidation & Fear** – Until recently they have used public displays of force, including arrests and interrogations, to keep the populace away from crime.
- **Militarization of the Republic** – They advocate for a stronger, more disciplined military presence to defend against both external threats and internal instability.

### Goals
- Protect the [[Republic of Bridgehold]] and its citizens from external threats and internal unrest.
- Ensure the republic’s military readiness against aggressors.
- Cultivate loyalty and respect for the republic's institutions among the population.
