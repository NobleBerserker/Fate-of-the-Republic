A cult that makes pacts with a number of stronger beings. They have found a loophole that if they make a pact with a new being, the new master has to pay the previous one and in exchange he receives a lot of followers since the cult has many members

**Methods:**
- Members initiate pacts with various entities—demons, devils, eldritch beings, or even rogue deities.
- They never remain loyal to a single patron for long, ensuring constant leverage.

Leadership: Led by the [[Binder]], they don't make deals for themselves but act as an intermediary.
