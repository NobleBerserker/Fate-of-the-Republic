A criminal organization
### Type
Gang of burglars, pickpockets, and thieves.

### Territory
Operates in [[Weaver’s Rest]].


###Goals
- **Stay Independent** – Refuse to be absorbed by larger criminal organizations like the **[[Grey Hand]]**, even if it means constant turf wars.
- Reclaim [[Ashen Fang (Dagger)|Ralindra’s Dagger]] and ring – During Ralindra's mourning the dagger was stolen along with her [[Veyn Wedding Ring|grandmother's wedding ring]].
### Rivalries
Clashes with the **[[Grey Hand]]**, as they refuse to be absorbed into its criminal network.
Clashes with [[Dark Blades]], for territory in the Weaver's rest.

Members often wear orange masks.
