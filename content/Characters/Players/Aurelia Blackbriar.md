[[House Blackbriar]] used to be a rich merchant family, trading in weapons made from metal mined locally and crafted by local blacksmiths. As the years went by, the Blackbriars gained almost a monopoly on weapon trade in the area (AND CALTROPS!), signing exclusive contracts with blacksmiths, etc.

20 years ago, a revolution transformed [[Bannershire]] into a republic. The Blackbriar family suffered significant losses during the revolution, but they remained wealthy. However, what they didn’t expect was that their enemies—the very ones who had orchestrated the revolution—were not content with just this. They wanted to claim all the family’s riches for themselves.
