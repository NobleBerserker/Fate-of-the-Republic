
Duke Alric Veymont was the last feudal ruler of Bannershire and the surrounding territories, which would eventually become the [[Republic of Bridgehold]]. Once regarded as a pragmatic and capable leader, his reign gradually descended into tyranny, corruption, and oppression, ultimately sparking the revolution that brought an end to his rule.

In his later years, Veymont imposed increasingly harsh taxes, demanded relentless military conscription, and granted extraordinary privileges to wealthy merchant families, one of which [[House Blackbriar|House Blackbriar]], whose control over the region's lucrative weapons trade helped finance his military ambitions.

As discontent spread, the Duke responded to protests with violence and public executions, turning an increasingly desperate population against him.

## The Fall of Duke Veymont

The revolution began with civil unrest in **[[Bannershire]]**, following a mysterious fire at a grain warehouse. Starving peasants stormed the local tax office, and the Duke's brutal response transformed scattered protests into organized resistance.

As the rebellion grew, revolutionary factions such as the **[[Iron Guard]]** and **[[The Iron Pact]]** emerged. Among the most prominent figures opposing Veymont were **[[Viktor Roscoe]]**, **[['One-Eyed' Jordan Trollsbane]]**, **[[Gareth Holloway]]**, and **[[Granny Ralindra|Ralindra Veyn]]**.

After years of conflict, the Duke's forces began to crumble. Facing defeat, Veymont retreated with his remaining loyal soldiers to **[[Castle Blackthorne]]**, intending to use the ancient fortress as his final stronghold against the advancing rebels.

However, Castle Blackthorne concealed dangers far greater than the Duke anticipated. Horrors lurking within its walls and beneath its foundations decimated his remaining soldiers, leaving the once-powerful ruler isolated and vulnerable.

When the revolutionaries finally reached Castle Blackthorne, they encountered the remnants of the Duke's failed defense. **[[Viktor Roscoe]]** ultimately captured Duke Alric Veymont within the fortress, bringing his desperate last stand to an end.

The Duke was subsequently taken into revolutionary custody and **publicly executed**, marking the definitive end of the old feudal regime.
