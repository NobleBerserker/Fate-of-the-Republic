# Lorian Veymont

**Lorian Veymont** is the Marshal of the [[Republic of Bridgehold]], commander of its armed forces, and the eldest son of [[Duke Alric Veymont]], the last Duke of Bannershire.

A seasoned warrior and skilled military commander, Lorian was raised to inherit his father's title. However, as Duke Alric's reign descended into tyranny, **Lorian turned against his own father**, abandoning his birthright to join the revolution. His military experience and knowledge of the Duke's forces proved invaluable to the rebels, eventually earning him a respected position within the newly established Republic.

As Marshal, Lorian oversees the Republic's military and is entrusted with the guardianship of [[Prince Dorian Valiros]], heir to the throne of the [[Kingdom of Aeloris]]. The prince resides under his supervision as a **hostage-student**, receiving military education while serving as a guarantee of peace between the two nations.

Stern, disciplined, and scarred by years of battle, Lorian is a man of honor who values duty above personal ambition. Though his family name remains associated with his father's tyranny, his actions during the revolution have earned him the respect of the Republic's people.
